# EnterLMS is an academy for Satriyo's AI product family

EnterLMS was positioned as an Indonesian banking/OJK compliance LMS. We are freezing that language and treating this product as an academy for people who run and build the AI product family (Enteraksi first). It is not a generic AI school and not a live-agent control plane.

The public catalog lists **Open Courses** only. v1: Pengenalan Agen AI (free, self-enroll). Restricted Courses (Administrasi Agen OpenClaw) stay off that catalog. Anyone who registers may become a Learner of an Open Course; that does not make them an Operator or unlock OpenClaw.

Operators walk a Learning Path: Pengenalan, then OpenClaw. LMS Admin grants Path enrollment. OpenClaw stays locked until Pengenalan is complete. Completing Pengenalan as a public Learner is not enough.

Tenant-specific Restricted Courses and unified Enteraksi login come later. Agent runtimes stay Course subjects; Enteraksi stays the control plane. LMS Admin in this phase is only the founder.

We rejected swapping only the seed catalog, becoming an AI-native lab platform, keeping banking as a second public track, and opening the whole academy (including OpenClaw) to the public.
