# The public catalog may list many free Open Courses

ADR 001 used “not a generic AI school” to keep the public catalog to one intro (Pengenalan Agen AI). That mixed two decisions: we are not a live-agent control plane, and we would not teach AI in the open catalog beyond one Course. The second was a catalog-size rule. It is retired.

The public catalog lists Open Courses only. They are free. A Learner may self-enroll. There may be many of them. Pengenalan Agen AI is the first, not a cap. Restricted Courses (Administrasi Agen OpenClaw) stay off that catalog. Completing an Open Course still does not grant Restricted. A Lesson is still not a console.

We rejected keeping a one-Course catalog as a brand guard, and rejected putting OpenClaw on the public catalog to “have more Courses.” More catalog means more Open Courses, not opening Restricted.
