# The app conforms to the Tenang hybrid, and drops what the frozen domain left behind

The redesign tournament produced a hybrid winner: Claude A's "Tenang" palette and type on Claude B's application shell. Tokens, fonts and the grouped sidebar shipped in `4af0917`, but the decision itself was recorded only in a comment at the top of `resources/css/app.css`. This ADR is that record.

Tenang's tokens were aliased onto the shadcn names — `--card` points at `--surface`, `--muted` at `--surface-2`, and so on. The aliasing was right, and it was also why the migration stalled: every existing `bg-card` kept painting the correct colour, so an unfinished migration looked finished and nothing forced it to completion. Three of 314 components used a Tenang-specific token. The rest were stock shadcn utilities that happened to resolve correctly.

Aliasing only holds where the two systems agree one-for-one. Where they disagree it silently picks the wrong value, which is equally invisible. Four such defects were fixed: `--secondary-foreground` carried full-strength ink onto Tenang's quiet surface, `--destructive-foreground` was a hardcoded white that measured 2.5:1 against dark mode's salmon `--danger` (now 7.0:1), `--sidebar-border` diverged between the theme bridge and the dark block, and the sidebar's outline variant wrapped hex tokens in `hsl()`, which produces nothing.

The larger gap was not in components at all. `lib/constants.ts`, `lib/formatters.ts` and `lib/icons.ts` each held a private table of stock Tailwind hues, and they disagreed: `draft` was grey in one and yellow in another, `completed` green in one and purple in another. The same status rendered a different colour depending on which helper a page happened to import. All three now resolve through one `TONES` table whose meanings are fixed — `neutral`, `primary`, `info`, `warn`, `danger`, `ok`, `gold` — and because the tokens behind them are redefined under `.dark`, every `dark:` variant in those tables was deleted rather than translated.

Gold means achievement and nothing else. A completed Enrollment is gold; a published Course is `ok`. That is the one place the palette carries meaning a reader could not guess from the hue.

We chose conformance before parity. Restyling the five hot primitives and the seven CRUD shells reaches 31 of the 54 pages at once, which restyling pages one at a time would not; `PageHeader` alone appears in 23. The 23 pages that import no shell — the public landing page and the course browse screens among them — carry their own headers and are unaffected. A Pest gate (`tests/Feature/Design/TenangConformanceTest.php`) holds the line with a baseline of the 70 page components that still use stock hues: that list may shrink, never grow, and the test fails in both directions so a cleaned file cannot quietly stay listed. `components/ui/` is exempt, because translating shadcn names into Tenang values is exactly its job.

Two things had to happen before any of this was worth doing. Roughly a third of the pages the tournament was designed against served concepts `CONTEXT.md` no longer names — payments, SCORM and the question bank were deleted rather than restyled. And the seven roles collapsed to Learner and LMS Admin.

That collapse cost two authorization boundaries, not just vocabulary, and we accepted both. Staff could previously edit only Courses they authored, and only while those Courses were drafts; with one staff role there is no second author to be isolated from, and `CoursePolicy::update` now returns true for any LMS Admin before the ownership branch is ever reached. The founder can also delete ratings on their own Courses, because they are both author and moderator. Neither is defensible in a multi-staff product; both are correct while the academy has one LMS Admin. The capability methods on `User` (`canManageCourses()`, `canGradeAssessments()`, …) exist as the seam where those grants change without touching every policy.

We rejected keeping both palettes behind a feature flag, restyling page-by-page from the most-visited down, and adopting Claude B's palette wholesale to avoid the migration entirely.
